Specifications In Depth

With an impressive top speed of roughly 160 mph and a dry weight of 364 lbs. (407 lbs. wet), the Daytona is no stranger to the wind. The chassis and frame were designed specifically for cornering on the track: nimble, light, and perfectly balanced, the corners are its dominion. With a wheel base of 54.8 inches, and a width of 28 inches, the Daytona is one of the most flickable machines around. As soon as an elbow begins to fall into a corner, they Daytona goes right along with it.
