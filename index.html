2006 Triumph Daytona 675

Overview

The 2006 Daytona 675 is British Manufacturer Triumph's, flagship SuperSport motorcycle. It boasts a horizontal-inline 3 cylinder motor producing 126 hp at 12,600 RPM and 54 ft. lb of torque at 11,750 RPM. This motorcycle was designed to compete against other SuperSport motorcycles in the 600cc class: Kawasaki Ninja ZX6R, Honda CBR 600rr, and Suzuki GSXR 600. While its competitors have 4 cylinder engines under their clothes, the 675 boasts the inline 3 cylinder, producing a unique sound, almost like a whistle at idle and high RPMs, that can only be described as "heavenly unique".
