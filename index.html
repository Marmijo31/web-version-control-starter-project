2006 Triumph Daytona 675

The 2006 Daytona 675 is British Manufacturer Triumph's, flagship SuperSport motorcycle. It boasts a horizontal-inline 3 cylinder motor producing 126 hp at 12,600 RPM and 54 ft. lb of torque at 11,750 RPM. This motorcycle was designed to compete against other SuperSport motorcycles in the 600cc class: Kawasaki Ninja ZX6R, Honda CBR 600, and Suzuki GSXR 600. While its competitors have 4 cylinder engines under their clothes, the 675 boasts the inline 3 cylinder, producing a unique sound, almost like a whistle at idle and high RPMs, that can only be described as "heavenly unique".

Specifications in Depth

With an impressive top speed of roughly 160 mph and a dry weight of 364 lbs. (407 lbs. wet), the Daytona is no stranger to the wind. The chassis and frame were designed specifically for cornering on the track: nimble, light, and perfectly balanced, the corners are its dominion. With a wheel base of 54.8 inches, and a width of 28 inches, the Daytona is one of the most flickable machines around. As soon as an elbow begins to fall into a corner, they Daytona goes right along with it.
